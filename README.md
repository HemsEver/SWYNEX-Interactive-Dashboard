# SWYNEX Technologies Power BI Internship - Task 3: Interactive Dashboard

## Project Overview
This repository contains the interactive Power BI dashboard developed for Task 3 of the SWYNEX Technologies Power BI Internship. Building upon the cleaned dataset (Task 1) and the Star Schema data model with DAX measures (Task 2), this report provides business stakeholders with an executive overview of Superstore sales and profitability performance.

## Dashboard Components
* **Executive KPI Cards:** Displays high-level performance metrics including Total Sales, Profit Margin (%), Total Profit, and Average Sales.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Enables dynamic filtering across geographic regions (`RegionTable[Region]`) and customer segments (`Cleaned_Superstore[Segment]`).
* **Category Performance Analysis:** A clustered column chart comparing Total Profit and Total Sales across Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture.
* **Regional Sales Distribution:** A donut chart illustrating revenue share and breakdown across the West, East, Central, and South regions.

## Key Business Insights
* **Overall Revenue & Profitability:** Across all segments, total revenue reaches $2.30M with $286.24K in net profit, yielding an overall profit margin of 12.47%.
* **Segment Performance:** Filtering by the Corporate segment accounts for $706.07K in sales and $91.95K in profit, achieving an above-average profit margin of 13.02%.
* **Category & Regional Trends:** Technology leads overall category revenue and profitability, while the West and East regions generate the largest share of total sales.
